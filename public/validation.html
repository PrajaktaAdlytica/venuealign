Expansion and commercial test

Distribution
Test distribution through rental houses and venue networks. With authorization, approved venue capability profiles could be reused by multiple tours.
Recurring demand
At the $18,000 annual hypothesis, 56 equivalent customers would produce $1,008,000 in annual recurring revenue. Validate reachable buyers, implementation cost and renewal before treating that arithmetic as a forecast.
Decision gate
Proceed beyond preview only when a budget owner funds the evaluation, the records owner authorizes data and a named reviewer accepts the comparison protocol.
