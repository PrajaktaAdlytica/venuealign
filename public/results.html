Measures to agree before the pilot

Review minutes

Time from opening a case to a recorded disposition. Agree when pauses start and stop. Compare minutes per case on matched inputs.

Valid substitutions

Count reviewer-accepted alternatives against the final rental amendments and technical decisions. Define eligible cases and the denominator up front.

Unsupported suggestions

Count suggested fits that lack sufficient support. Record missed conflicts, false clearance and their consequences alongside speed.
