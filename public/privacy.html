Local decisions

Interaction events
The site records event names and timestamps locally for product views, walkthrough starts, reviewer interactions, pilot interest and qualified pilot briefs. Export is local. Clear this preview’s local data below.
Hosting
The hosting platform handles requests for site assets. A funded deployment needs its own reviewed retention, access and privacy terms.
