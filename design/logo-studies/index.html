Reference notes

Shakespeare’s Globe printed its symbol from a block of wood, keeping the grain visible. House Fold takes its shape from a curtain pulled by hand. Design Bridge and Partners

Williamstown Theatre Festival’s identity draws on stagecraft and the shape of a stage. Portal Register takes its proportions from VenueAlign’s front-elevation drawings. Pentagram

Atlantic Theater’s emblem reads as an A and evokes a spotlight or megaphone. The Reviewer’s Hand uses that principle of a second reading for a VA monogram that also feels like a signature. Pentagram
