## Remaining gates

- **Public acquisition launch.** Confirm the public host and domain, connect lead delivery and consent handling, and configure measurement for real traffic. Pilot, demo and contact forms currently make local downloads. Interaction events stay on the device.
- **Live product.** Implement workspace authentication and access controls, permissioned document ingestion, extraction and matching, and durable shared review history. The preview illustrates AI-assisted findings with fictional records.
- **Commercial validation.** Obtain the funded scope, authorized baseline files and a named reviewer per participating stop. Agree thresholds and review adverse outcomes with the buyer. Neither the proposed annual price nor the $100,000 platform ambition is a validated valuation.
